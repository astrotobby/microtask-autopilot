✓ APPLICATION PACKET

Ready means “ready for you to review.”

The private packet builder blocks readiness when required checks are incomplete. This is the implemented checklist; no personal answers are shown here.

  1. 01Eligibility is establishedUnsupported or expired listings are blocked; unknown country status stays a manual check.
  2. 02Required profile answers are completeMissing personal facts are never invented or published in this view.
  3. 03Draft is complete and approved by the userUnresolved placeholders and an unapproved draft keep the packet blocked.
  4. 04Deadline and qualifications are reviewedExpired deadlines and qualification gaps are surfaced before the human applies.
Final action: the user applies themselves. Generating a packet never sends an application.
! PLATFORM-RISK REVIEW

Signals to pause on—not a legitimacy stamp.

The repository's deterministic review flags patterns in listing text and source metadata. A low score does not prove a platform is safe.

Up-front fee or depositUnrealistic guaranteed incomeRequests for passwords or one-time codesSuspicious transfers or crypto requestsUnsecured or mismatched source domainPayment not disclosed
High risk: pause and do not proceed until independently resolved.
Needs review: check source identity, terms and details yourself.
The review cannot guarantee legitimacy, employment, earnings or payout.

SAFETY BOUNDARIES

Automation stops where your account begins.

Microtask Autopilot is a decision-support and record-keeping tool. It does not take control of platform accounts or perform work on a user's behalf.

01Public sources only

Automated discovery is limited to a source with an explicitly supported public interface.

02No logged-in activity

No account scraping, account creation, or automated work inside contributor dashboards.

03No task or identity actions

No accepting or completing tasks, submitting evidence, or bypassing identity, MFA or CAPTCHA checks.

04Private means private

No local profile, application, task, evidence, payment or notification records are exposed here.
